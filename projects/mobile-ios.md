## Industrial Scan Twin

[Repository](https://github.com/aminzoroufi/industrial-scan-twin) · **Early local MVP**

An iPhone scanning workflow for room geometry, asset labels, annotations and exports, with a local backend and browser review interface.

- **Focus:** RoomPlan/ARKit integration, SwiftUI workflow, scan data, annotations and export/synchronization.
- **Stack:** Swift, SwiftUI, ARKit, RoomPlan, RealityKit, Vision and Node.js.
- **Code to inspect:** [iOS app source](https://github.com/aminzoroufi/industrial-scan-twin/tree/main/ios/IndustrialTwinScanner/IndustrialTwinScanner).
- **Status:** Early MVP with a local workflow. A finished reconstruction pipeline, production deployment and real industrial device control are not included in the documented implementation.
